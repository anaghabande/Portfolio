Chile Tourism Decision System

Built a decision framework for 15 regions using 131 indicators, forecasting, digital signals, and two LINGO models with 165 decision variables.

+
Business problem
Allocate a USD 45 million tourism fund and route 110,000 high-season visitors without treating every region the same.

Evidence
15 regions · 131 indicators · 75 + 90 decisions · 71 + 37 constraints
Business relevance
Shows how transparent analytics can turn public investment, capacity, and equity constraints into an auditable decision.

Why the Same Speaker Can Win Alone and Lose Together

Designed and analyzed two online experiments testing evaluability and celebrity cues in product choice.

+
Business problem
Understand whether comparison format and an extrinsic cue change willingness to pay for technically different products.
Approach
Collected data from 111 respondents in nine countries and used SPSS t-tests plus robustness checks.
Evidence
111 respondents · 9 countries · 4 conditions · SPSS
Business relevance
The hypotheses were not supported; reporting the null result and its limitations produced more credible marketing guidance.

Dragon Fruit–Fortified Shrikhand

Developed and evaluated a fortified dairy product using dragon fruit pulp and fructooligosaccharide as a sugar substitute.

+
Business problem
Create a more nutritionally differentiated shrikhand while preserving sensory acceptability.
Approach
Tested 0%, 5%, 10%, and 15% dragon-fruit formulations, followed by physicochemical, sensory, color, and texture analysis.

Business relevance
Connects food-process design, quality measurement, and consumer acceptance.
