Business problem
How can AI-informed procurement improve efficiency without overlooking inclusion across agricultural markets?
Approach
Structured a market-level procurement analysis across 6,639 agricultural markets in India.
Analysis
Compared procurement considerations through the linked lenses of market reach, efficiency, and inclusion.
Key insight
Efficiency and inclusion must be treated as explicit procurement design tradeoffs.
Recommendation
Developed AI-driven procurement recommendations that keep inclusion visible in the decision.
Tools
Market analysis · AI-informed decision framework · Procurement analysis

Business relevance Demonstrates how analytics can support more considered procurement and supply-chain decisions at scale.
